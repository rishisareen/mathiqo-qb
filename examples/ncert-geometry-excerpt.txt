# Shapes, lines, angles, constructions and symmetry, Classes 1-6:
#
# Class 1-2, Maths (Joyful Mathematics): recognises and names basic 2-D
# shapes (circle, triangle, square, rectangle) in the environment;
# distinguishes straight and curved lines.
# Class 4, Maths (Maths Mela), Ch. 1 (Shapes Around Us): describes 2-D
# shapes by their sides and corners; distinguishes squares, rectangles,
# triangles and circles by their properties.
# Class 4, Maths (Maths Mela), Ch. 11 (Fun with Symmetry): identifies
# mirror halves of figures and completes a figure given half and the
# mirror line; finds lines of symmetry by paper folding.
# Class 5, Maths (Maths Mela), Ch. 3 (Angles as Turns): understands an
# angle as an amount of turn; identifies right angles and classifies
# angles as more or less than a right angle by comparison.
# Class 5, Maths (Maths Mela), Ch. 10 (Symmetrical Designs): creates and
# analyses symmetrical designs; identifies multiple lines of symmetry in
# shapes and patterns.
# Class 6, Maths (Ganita Prakash), Ch. 2 (Lines and Angles): identifies and
# names points, line segments, rays and lines; understands an angle as
# formed by two rays from a common vertex; compares angles and measures
# them in degrees using a protractor; draws an angle of a given measure;
# classifies angles as acute, right, obtuse, straight and reflex.
# Class 6, Maths (Ganita Prakash), Ch. 8 (Playing with Constructions):
# uses a ruler and compass to draw circles of a given radius, identifying
# the centre, radius and diameter; constructs squares and rectangles of
# given side lengths; creates and reasons about figures and curve-based
# art built from circles and line segments.
# Class 6, Maths (Ganita Prakash), Ch. 9 (Symmetry): identifies lines of
# symmetry (reflection symmetry) in 2-D figures, including figures with
# several lines of symmetry; completes figures given a line of symmetry;
# understands rotational symmetry and finds the order of rotational
# symmetry of a figure.
