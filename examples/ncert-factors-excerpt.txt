#

# multiples of whole numbers, recognising that a factor divides a number
# exactly and a multiple contains it exactly; identifies common factors and
# common multiples of two numbers, including the smallest common multiple
# and the highest common factor; distinguishes prime numbers from composite
# numbers and explains why 1 is neither; expresses a number as a product of
# prime factors (prime factorisation); applies divisibility tests for 2, 4,
# 5, 8 and 10 by examining the digits of a number; explores prime patterns
# and puzzles (e.g. twin primes, Goldbach-style questions) informally.
