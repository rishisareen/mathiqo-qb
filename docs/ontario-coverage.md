## Syllabus skeleton available from maplemath (not verified)

The maplemath repo (`goaisolutions/maplemath`) stores, for every chapter in grades 1-9, a list of Ontario 2020 expectation codes (`ontario_expectations`, for example `B1.3`, `C3.2`, `F1.4`, `E2.5`) in `wp-content/plugins/mm-story-engine/content/gradeN/unit*.php`. It also holds `mm_get_ontario_expectations()` in `wp-content/themes/maplemath/functions.php`, with paraphrased expectation text (only grade 5 was inspected).

Usable as: a first-draft chapter-to-code skeleton to speed up an Ontario teacher's review.
Not usable as: authoritative expectation text, grade placement, or question content. Those need the official Ontario curriculum and a named reviewer.
